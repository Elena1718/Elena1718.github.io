A Little Bit About Me

I was born in Congo, but i grow up in brazil, and I have been living in the United States for the past 4 years. I graduated from high school in 2025, and I am currently pursuing a degree in Cybersecurity. As someone who have a life of contantly moving and adapting to new environments, I have developed a strong sense of resilience and adaptability. Though my jorney I had to find new ways to learn new languages and cultures, which has helped me develop a unique perspective on the world. And such experience gave me the ability to connect with people from diverse backgrounds and understand their perspectives.

As someone who grow up in a family of 7 with 4 siblings, I have learned the importance of communication, collaboration, and empathy. I have also developed a strong work ethic and a sense of responsibility, as I have had to contribute to the household and support my family in various ways. Being an older sister has also taught me the importance of patience, understanding, and guidance, as I have had to help my younger siblings in different ways many times being school related.

I have always been passionate about learning, I believe that in a world where evryhting can be taken from you, knowledge is the one thing that can never be taken away. I have always been curious about the world around me and have sought out opportunities to expand my knowledge and skills. I enjoy exploring new ideas, learning about different cultures, and discovering new ways to approach problems. I am also passionate about sharing my knowledge with others, which is what led me to become a tutor. I believe that education is a powerful tool that can help individuals achieve their goals and reach their full potential. As a tutor, I am committed to providing personalized instruction that meets the unique needs of each student, and I strive to create a supportive and engaging learning environment that encourages students to ask questions, explore new ideas, and develop a love of learning. If they want to learn a new language I can help with that and provide them with the tools and resources they need to succeed. while allowing them to know the strugues that comes with it, but also the rewards of learning a new language and how it can open up new opportunities and experiences. If they strugle with a subject I can help them understand the concepts and provide them with strategies to improve their performance.

That is me and everything I have been through and learned in my life has shaped me into the person I am today, and I am excited to continue learning and growing as a tutor and as an individual. I hope I can share and help my students trough their academic journey.
