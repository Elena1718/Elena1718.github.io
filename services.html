What I Teach

In-Person Tutoring Sessions

My services provide personalized one-on-one instruction to help students grasp complex concepts and improve academic confidence. You provide the location, and I come to you for our session

Sessions run between 1 to 2 hours. There are no additional travel fees for locations within my standard service area in Maine (up to 2 hours away).

Subjects Offered:

  • Math: Foundation skills, arithmetic, pre-algebra, and algebra prep.
  • Science: General science, biology fundamentals, and earth science.
  • English: Reading comprehension, grammar, and writing composition.
  • Spanish: Beginners to intermediate conversational and written Spanish.
  • Portuguese: Beginner to advanced conversation and grammar.
